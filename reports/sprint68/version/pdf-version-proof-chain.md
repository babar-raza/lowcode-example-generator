## Proof Chain

### Step 1: Handoff Directory.Packages.props

```xml

  <PropertyGroup>
    <ManagePackageVersionsCentrally>true</ManagePackageVersionsCentrally>

```

**Evidence**: `<PackageVersion Include="Aspose.PDF" Version="26.5.0" />` — version 26.5.0 is the NuGet
reference used by all 19 PDF handoff examples.

### Step 2: pr-dry-run Packages

The `workspace/pr-dry-run/pdf-controlled-pilot-pr7/` through `pdf-controlled-pilot-pr9/` directories
also reference `Version="26.5.0"`. These were generated by the pipeline after the version bump.

Earlier pr-dry-run batches (pr5, pr6, wave1, wave2) show 26.4.0 — these were generated before
the Sprint 58 version bump and are not the current handoff packages.

### Step 3: Sprint 67 Version Decision

File: `reports/sprint67/version/pdf-version-decision.md`

Sprint 67 established the HANDOFF_CANONICAL policy: version authority is the handoff
`Directory.Packages.props`. The handoff props shows 26.5.0. Therefore 26.5.0 is canonical.

### Step 4: Content Audit Alignment

Sprint 67's `content-audit-sprint67.json` records `package_version: "26.5.0"` for all 19 PDF types.
Sprint 68's `content-audit-sprint68.json` continues this with `version_status: "POLICY_CONFIRMED_26_5_0"`.

### Step 5: NuGet Published Version

Aspose.PDF 26.5.0 is a published NuGet package. The handoff `Directory.Packages.props`
references the exact published version. NuGet restore will download and use 26.5.0.
