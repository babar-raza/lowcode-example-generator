# Delegated Authority Proof

Human delegated safe technical, content, fixture, classification, PR-publication,
and merge-readiness decisions to the agent.

Delegated scope:
- PR creation: AUTHORIZED (exercised in prior sprint)
- PR merge: AUTHORIZED (delegated in current session)
- Branch deletion after merge: AUTHORIZED
- Excluded-item reaudit decisions: AUTHORIZED
- Fixture system design: AUTHORIZED

Not delegated:
- External credentials
- Legal/provenance decisions
- Irreversible actions blocked by repo policy
